What this scan can and can’t tell you

Bodyguard fetches the public page and applies local security heuristics. It does not execute the site’s JavaScript, use a commercial malware or phishing reputation database, or certify a site as safe. “No major issues found” means this scan did not observe its listed warning signs; stay cautious with unfamiliar sites.
